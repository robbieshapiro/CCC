## Decided, not built yet

### Weekly play (live from Fri Oct 30)

The problem with the new members: once your best round is in, there's no reason to come back, and one course for a whole month goes stale. The fix is a new course every week.

- **A week runs Sunday to Saturday** and closes at midnight ET going into Sunday. The best round of the week wins, and the winner goes on the plaque.
- **No monthly title.** Weekly wins are the titles. October closed early, at midnight going into Fri Oct 30 (Marc leads Kellogg and the Open at -6).
- **Courses are 3 holes,** like the Highlands. A round takes about 5 minutes.
- **One card a day counts** (from Nov 8, #149). Practice rounds of the week's course are unlimited and never count.
- **Stroke play only for now.** Skins (each hole goes to the outright lowest score on it that week) may come later. No handicaps.
- **Gear unlocks count weekly wins** at higher thresholds (something like 1, 3, 5, 10, 20), since there'll be about four titles a month.
- **The Open goes weekly too:** one board across every club for the week's course. From Nov 8 it's the main board and pays the only purse (#151); each club still crowns its own champion.
- **If a week's course isn't ready,** the last one carries over. That fallback already works for months.

**Schedule:**

## The economy (from #161)

What a week is worth, roughly, with 25 players on the Open:

| Member | Purse | Daily bonuses and moments | A week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wins the week | about $1,900 | $700 to $1,000 | about $2,800 |
| Regular, top 10 now and then | about $350 | $500 to $700 | about $950 |
| Casual, three cards a week | rarely | $200 to $300 | about $250 |

So Club Pro ($5,000) takes a winner about 2 weeks, a regular about 5, a casual member most of a season; Club Legend ($25,000 and a title) takes the best player about 9 weeks; the caddie ($50,000) is a season-long chase. Shop floor pieces ($150 to $1,500) are a week or two for anyone; the Pro rack ($1,500 to $6,000) one every few weeks for a regular; the Legend rack ($10,000 to $30,000) one every month or two for the best. Only the daily card earns, so playing more rounds doesn't. If the club passes about 60 players, look again at the $250.
